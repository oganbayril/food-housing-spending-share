# food-housing-spending-share

Share of household consumption spending that goes to food and to housing &
utilities across OECD and European countries, over time.

Work in progress. Full data notes are in [DATA_NOTES.md](DATA_NOTES.md).

## Data caveats (so far)

All figures are official statistics from Eurostat and the OECD.

**United Kingdom**
- The UK is the only European country taken from the OECD instead of
  Eurostat, because Eurostat's UK series ends in 2019. The whole UK series
  (1995-2025) comes from the OECD, so the two sources are never mixed within
  it. The map's hover shows the source.
- Where both sources publish UK figures (1995-2019), they differ by about
  0.5 percentage points for housing and up to 0.9 points for food. The cause
  of the difference has not been established, so UK values carry this extra
  uncertainty.
- Checked before mapping: the OECD's UK series uses the same classification
  for all years (COICOP 1999) and the same definition of total spending as
  Eurostat (all household spending on the country's territory).
